# Ancient Witnesses — content provenance

Ancient Witnesses grew from **73 verses to 3166** across two sourcing passes.
All added texts are **public-domain** translations, fetched from
earlychristianwritings.com / en.wikisource.org / archive.org and spot-verified
against known landmark passages.

## Apostolic Fathers — Roberts-Donaldson (Ante-Nicene Fathers, 1885, public domain)
| Book | Chapters | Verses | Landmark verified |
|------|---------:|-------:|-------------------|
| Didache | 16 | 134 | Eucharist prayer ("broken bread… scattered over the hills") |
| 1 Clement | 59 | 388 | Peter/Paul martyrdom; the phoenix (ch 25) |
| Epistle of Barnabas | 21 | 427 | "two ways… light… darkness" (ch 18); eighth-day/Sabbath (ch 15) |
| Shepherd of Hermas | 27 | 1,580 | opening "sold me to one Rhode in Rome"; Mandate 1; Similitudes |
| Letters of Ignatius (all 7) | 7 | 458 | "I am the wheat of God…" (to the Romans) |
| Epistle to Diognetus | 12 | 134 | "Christians dwell in the world, yet are not of the world" (ch 6) |

Shepherd of Hermas = Visions (ch 1–5) + Commandments/Mandates (ch 6–17) +
Similitudes (ch 18–27); 59 all-caps editorial "argument" headings and 2
drop-cap artifacts were removed; the translation's own idiosyncrasies are
preserved verbatim.

## Non-Christian testimonia (pre-existing)
Josephus, Tacitus, Pliny the Younger, Suetonius, Mara bar Serapion, Lucian —
short by nature (these are the brief external references to Jesus/early
Christians).

## Gospel of Thomas — REPLACED for copyright
The previous text was the **Lambdin** translation (post-1945 Coptic find,
**copyrighted**). It was removed and replaced with the genuinely public-domain
**Grenfell & Hunt** translation (1897–1904) of the Greek **Oxyrhynchus**
fragments (P.Oxy. 654, 1, 655) — 14 sayings, landmark "Raise the stone… cleave
the wood" (Logion 5) verified. This is fragmentary (the complete 114-saying
Coptic Thomas has no public-domain translation), but it is legally clean and
historically primary.
