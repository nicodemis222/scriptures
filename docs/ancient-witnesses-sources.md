## Not added (integrity hold)
- **Epistle of Barnabas** and **Shepherd of Hermas** — the automated fetch could
  not return faithful *verbatim* text (Barnabas's supersessionist passages were
  summarized rather than quoted; Hermas was content-filtered). Rather than insert
  paraphrased or uncertain text into a scripture reference, these two empty stub
  books were **removed**. They can be re-added later from a verified PD source.
- **Gospel of Thomas** remains the pre-existing 12-saying excerpt. Note: that
  text appears to be the Lambdin translation, which is **copyrighted** — it should
  be replaced with a public-domain translation or licensed before wide release.
